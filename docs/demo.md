# WAYOS PREP — Demo Script

## Setup

1. Start backend: `cd infra && docker compose up --build`
2. Start frontend: `cd frontend && npx expo start`
3. Open Expo Go on your phone and scan the QR code

## Demo Flow

### Demo 1: Prep This Account (Primary Demo)

1. Open the WAYOS PREP app
2. Tap **Prep This Account**
3. Select **Roofing** from the industry picker
4. Enter Location: **North Carolina**
5. Enter Employees: **12**
6. Enter MOD: **1.15**
7. Enter Vehicle Exposure: **3 trucks**
8. Tap **Generate Brief**
9. Review the full Client Brief
10. Tap **Copy Brief** to copy to clipboard
11. Tap **Send Pack**
12. Tap **Send Underwriter Email** — shows a ready-to-send email
13. Go back, tap **Send Internal Note** — shows a CSR-ready summary
14. Rate the brief with the feedback buttons

### Demo 2: Ask Risk Question

1. From Home, tap **Ask Risk Question**
2. Type: "What risks should I discuss with a trucking company?"
3. Optionally add location: "Texas"
4. Tap **Generate Brief**
5. Review the generated brief
6. Share via Send Pack

### Demo 3: Industry Lookup

1. From Home, tap **Industry Lookup**
2. Browse the list of 20 industries
3. Search for "restaurant"
4. Tap **Restaurants**
5. Review the full risk profile
6. Tap **Generate Brief** from the detail screen

## Demo Data Highlights

- **Roofing**: Falls from height, hail belt exposure, completed operations
- **Trucking**: Nuclear verdicts, CSA scores, cargo liability
- **Restaurants**: Liquor liability, foodborne illness, kitchen burns
- **Daycare**: Abuse/molestation coverage, staff ratios, transport liability
- **Assisted Living**: Patient lifting injuries, elopement risk, medication errors
